Dehna (ደህና), the health-access app of Han's With Care Manufacturing, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Englishአማርኛ

Last updated 27 October 2026. This page is the privacy policy for the Dehna website (dehna.hanswithcare.com) and the Dehna Android app (package com.hanswithcare.dehna). It is also available inside the app under Menu → Privacy notice.

1. Who we are

Dehna is a service owned and operated by Han's With Care Manufacturing, 4 Kilo (Arat Kilo), Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Contact for privacy questions and requests: wearelistening@hanswithcare.com, +251 91 263 7344.

2. The short version

We collect the minimum needed. You can browse directories and call emergency numbers without an account. A phone number, your consent and a location are requested only when you send a request or an order. Health information you choose to write in a request is shared only with the responder you choose, and is deleted according to our retention schedule. You can ask for access, correction or deletion at any time.

3. What we collect and why

DataWhenWhy
Anonymous device identifier (a random token stored on your phone)Always, when you use the appTo show you your own requests and orders without an account
Language and region preference, offline packs you downloadWhen you choose themTo show content in your language and make it work offline

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising trackers. The app does not request access to your contacts, photos or files. Location is requested only when you tap a button that needs it, and you can choose an area manually instead.

4. Who sees your information

A request is visible only to you, to the responder you selected (and, if you agree to a referral, the professional it is referred to), and to Han's With Care's authorised safety and administration staff. Emergency numbers and directories are public information and involve no personal data.

5. Where data is stored

During the current pilot, data is processed on secure cloud servers operated for Han's With Care in the European Union (Frankfurt), protected by encryption in transit and access controls. Han's With Care plans to host data in Ethiopia as the service grows, in line with Ethiopian law. We will update this notice when that happens.

6. How long we keep it

Requests, orders and related messages are kept only as long as needed to provide the service and handle complaints, then deleted according to our retention schedule. Professional account and licence records are kept for as long as the account is active and for the period required by the regulator. You can ask us to delete your data earlier (see section 8).

7. Children

Dehna is intended for adults and for young people seeking health information with a caregiver's support. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data linked to your phone number or account. Send your request to wearelistening@hanswithcare.com or use Menu → My data in the app. We verify your identity before responding. You can also raise a complaint or safety concern under Menu → Complaint.

9. Changes

We will post changes to this notice on this page and in the app, with the date of the last update.
